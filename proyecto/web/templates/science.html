The Science Behind MammoPatch

When a lactating milk duct experiences stasis or blockage, the epidermal tissue resting directly over it registers a distinct thermal elevation. This occurs significantly earlier than symptomatic pain. MammoPatch utilizes this biomedical phenomenon to empower mothers to act proactively.

Advanced Wearable Architecture

MammoPatch integrates state-of-the-art engineering into an incredibly discrete form factor. The exterior shell utilizes a highly flexible, bio-compatible TPU polymer, guaranteeing skin safety and structural durability against moisture.

Safely encased within this soft exterior is an ultra-thin flexible printed circuit board (PCB). This board houses an array of high-precision AMG8833 infrared temperature sensors, low-emission indicator micro-LEDs, and specialized vibration motors designed to deliver precise mechanical relief without disturbing the mother's daily routine.

The Quadrant Mapping Differential

To provide accurate alerts, MammoPatch continuously scans four distinct anatomical zones (quadrants) of the breast.

Because every woman's baseline body temperature is unique and fluctuates naturally, an absolute temperature threshold is medically insufficient. Instead, MammoPatch employs a differential algorithmic approach. If the sensors calculate that one specific quadrant is sustaining a temperature roughly 1.2 °C higher than the surrounding quadrants, the internal microcontroller registers a high probability of localized inflammation and immediately triggers the therapeutic response protocol.

Secure Data Transmission Protocol

Our robust functional prototype utilizes a highly efficient ESP32 microcontroller system. This chip is responsible for rapidly processing the thermal array data and transmitting it wirelessly to the user dashboard.

Patient privacy is paramount. All data transmitted from the MammoPatch wearable to the cloud infrastructure utilizes end-to-end encryption protocols, assuring total compliance with stringent European data protection frameworks (GDPR) prior to reaching your personal device screen.
