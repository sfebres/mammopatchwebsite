Caring early, before it hurts.

Between 1 in 10 and 1 in 3 breastfeeding mothers (10–33%) experience mastitis, a painful breast inflammation. It is most common in the first 12 weeks after birth. Most mothers only receive help once the pain, redness, or fever has already escalated significantly.

MammoPatch is designed to alter that timeline.

When a milk duct is blocked, the skin over it becomes slightly warmer—usually by about 1 to 2 °C. This thermal shift happens often a day or two (24-48 hours) before any pain sets in. MammoPatch identifies that early localized warmth, highlights the affected quadrant, and initiates a gentle vibration to naturally encourage milk flow.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the vibration safe for inflamed tissue?
Yes. It is engineered to be extremely gentle, matching the intensity of a soft electric toothbrush, which is explicitly recommended by our consulting gynecologist. Deep, aggressive massage on an already inflamed breast can exacerbate tissue damage, so the MammoPatch keeps stimulation very light. You should immediately cease use and consult a professional if symptoms progress to systemic fever, severe localized pain, skin redness, or purulent discharge.
Can I comfortably wear the patch while nursing or pumping?
MammoPatch features an ultra-thin profile, specifically designed to be easily slipped out of the bra cup prior to latching or attaching a breast pump flange. Its flexibility ensures it won't interfere with your standard maternity wear throughout the day.
Will the device fit securely in my specific nursing bra?
It is anatomically sculpted to rest inside standard maternity and nursing bras and can be effectively worn on either breast. The pliable, soft TPU casing naturally conforms to varied anatomical shapes.
Does the device emit any harmful radiation or heat?
Absolutely not. The internal array utilizes strictly passive infrared sensors. It solely reads the thermal energy naturally emitted by your skin. Furthermore, extensive thermal regulation safety tests ensure the exterior surface of the patch remains safely below 41 °C at all times.
Is my biometric data visible to my healthcare provider?
Yes, that is a core feature. The secure web dashboard dynamically graphs temperature trends and maintains a precise log of automated massages. This allows you to review historical data alongside your midwife or lactation consultant. You can also manually input secondary symptoms (like pain severity or redness) into the integrated digital diary.
Does this replace clinical diagnosis or treat mastitis?
No. MammoPatch is strictly an early-warning preventative aid. It does not diagnose medical conditions and it does not cure established infections. If you experience a high fever, intense debilitating pain, hard distinct lumps, or pus, you must contact your medical provider immediately.
