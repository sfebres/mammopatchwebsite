Supporting new mothers through gentle innovation.

The core objective of MammoPatch is to empower breastfeeding mothers by identifying blocked milk ducts at the earliest possible stage, significantly reducing the risk of developing painful clinical mastitis.

By seamlessly merging highly accurate thermal sensors, bio-compatible flexible materials, and an incredibly intuitive digital interface, we have designed a preventative tool that operates silently in the background of a mother's demanding schedule.

An Empowering Dashboard

Our sophisticated web architecture continuously graphs thermal variances across the entire breast. When the algorithmic threshold (ΔT ≥ 1.2 °C) is met, the system instantly logs a visual alert and records the duration of the automated therapeutic massage.

The user interface is intentionally engineered to remain clean, accessible, and devoid of medical clutter, ensuring that both mothers and lactation specialists can interpret the biometric data rapidly without feeling overwhelmed.

Our Guiding Engineering Principles

Absolute Comfort

From the precise material selection of the soft TPU shell to the calibrated intensity of the micro-vibration motors, every specification is rigorously tested to ensure prolonged physical comfort and absolute skin safety for lactating mothers.

Clinical Collaboration

Our core safety algorithms and massage cessation protocols were developed via continuous feedback loops with professional gynecologists and specialized maternity nurses. MammoPatch bridges the gap between hardware engineering and genuine clinical utility.

Unyielding Privacy

We respect the sensitive nature of biometric data. All thermal readings and behavioral logs transmitted by the device utilize modern encryption standards, ensuring our entire hardware-software ecosystem is fully compliant with comprehensive GDPR regulations.
